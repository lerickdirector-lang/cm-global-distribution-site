Questions we’re often asked

Which businesses do you supply?

Approved retailers, wholesalers and distributors, each vetted before the account opens. We don’t supply consumers, and every order is placed through an approved trade account.

Do you support market entry for brands new to the UK?

Yes. We import from the EU and supply trade accounts across the UK, so we can be your route into the UK market. You set the channels and the pace, and we build from there.

Which territories do you ship to?

The whole of the UK, including Northern Ireland. Delivery is quoted with each order.

Do you have a MOQ?

Yes. The minimum order quantity depends on the product and the brand, and it’s shown on your quote. Prices are set for carton quantities and above.
