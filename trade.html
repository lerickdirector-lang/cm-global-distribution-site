Trade terms

How pricing, order size, payment and delivery work on a trade account.

Pricing

Tiered by volume.

Unit prices fall as quantities rise, and regular buyers can be set account pricing. Price lists go to approved accounts and show only what’s in stock.

Order size

From carton quantities.

The MOQ is set per product and shown on your quote. Lines and pack sizes can be mixed where stock allows, and full pallets can be built on request.

Payment

Agreed per account, in writing.

Terms are set when the account opens, based on order size and trading history, and reviewed as we trade. Every invoice is a VAT invoice, and nothing is added once a quote is agreed.

Delivery

Across the UK.

Northern Ireland included. Every order ships tracked with its invoice and delivery note, and the lead time and cost are confirmed before you commit.

How to buy

We don’t run a public shop. Trade is by account, set up once and then used as often as you need.

  1. Tell us what you need

Questions trade buyers ask first

Which businesses do you supply?

Approved retailers, wholesalers and distributors, each vetted before the account opens. We don’t supply consumers, and every order is placed through an approved trade account.

Do brands set conditions on where their stock can be sold?

Some do. Where a brand sets conditions on where its stock can be sold, those conditions apply to your account, and we confirm them with your quote before you order.

Which brands are available to account holders?

Availability changes with each intake, so we don’t publish a brand list. Your price list shows current stock and volumes.

Do you publish a trade price list?

Not publicly. Pricing moves with stock and volume, so current price lists are issued to approved accounts. Indicative pricing is often available while an enquiry is being reviewed.

Which territories do you ship to?

The whole of the UK, including Northern Ireland. Delivery is quoted with each order.

Where is your stock sourced from?

Directly from brands, and from distributors a brand has appointed. If a line can’t be traced back to the brand, we don’t buy it.
