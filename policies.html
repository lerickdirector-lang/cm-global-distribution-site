Website terms

Who we are

This website is run by Crep Millions LTD, trading as CM Global Distribution, a company registered in England and Wales (number 13764987) with its registered office at 4th Floor, Silverstream House, 45 Fitzroy Street, London W1T 6EB.

What the site is for

The site is for businesses: brands looking for a distribution partner and trade buyers looking for stock. It is not a shop, and nothing on it is an offer to sell.

Enquiries are not orders

Sending an enquiry or applying for a trade account doesn’t create any agreement to supply. Supply only happens under terms we have both agreed in writing, and we may decline an application.

Using the content

The text, design and images on this site belong to us or the people who licensed them to us. Please don’t copy them, or collect data from the site automatically, without our written permission.

Accuracy

We keep the site accurate and up to date, but product availability, prices and lead times are only confirmed on a quote or order.

Translations

The site can be read in several languages for convenience. If a translation differs from the English, the English version applies.

Law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.

Privacy notice

Who is responsible for your data

Crep Millions LTD, trading as CM Global Distribution, is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us at info@cmglobaldistribution.co.uk.

What we collect

  • The details you give us in the enquiry form: your name, phone number, email address, company, website, VAT and company numbers, and what your business needs
  • Anything else you send us by email
  • For trade accounts, the business and order details we need to supply you and meet our legal obligations

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, check that a business is eligible for a trade account, and run the account once it’s open. We rely on our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries, and on taking steps towards a contract you’ve asked about. We don’t send marketing, and we never sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • The service that delivers form submissions to our inbox
  • Delivery companies, for orders
  • Our accountants and professional advisers, who must keep it confidential
  • Authorities, where the law requires it

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it. Enquiries that don’t lead to an account are deleted within 12 months. Account and order records are kept for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it. Email us and we’ll respond within one month. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This site doesn’t use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and our fonts are served from our own site rather than a third party. If that ever changes, we’ll update this section and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.
