Sourcing and provenance

All topics
Where is your stock sourced from?

Directly from brands, and from distributors a brand has appointed. If a line can’t be traced back to the brand, we don’t buy it.

What supply-chain documentation do you hold?

For each line: the brand’s authorisation, the supplier invoice, the written terms for your account, and the delivery note. Proof of provenance is available on request.

Do you trade in grey-market stock or parallel imports?

No. Grey-market stock, meaning goods sold outside a brand’s approved channels, and parallel imports are never part of an order.

Account opening

All topics
Which businesses do you supply?

Registered businesses buying for resale, including wholesalers, distributors and retailers. We don’t supply consumers, and every order is placed through an approved trade account.

What information do you need to open an account?

To enquire: your name, email, company name and company registration number, your type of business, what you buy, where you need delivery, a rough monthly volume, and a line on how we can help. Your VAT number is optional at that stage, and we’ll ask for it before the account opens if it’s missing.

What is the account-opening process?

Send an enquiry through the contact page, or email us directly. A member of the team reviews every enquiry personally and will set out anything further needed to open your account.

Ordering

All topics
Do you have a MOQ?

Yes. The minimum order quantity depends on the product and the brand, and it’s shown on your quote. Prices are set for carton quantities and above.

Which brands are available to account holders?

Availability changes with each intake, so we don’t publish a brand list. Your price list shows current stock and volumes.

Can orders be consolidated across multiple brands?

Yes, subject to stock. Your account contact confirms what can be combined on a single order.

Do you offer bespoke sourcing?

Where there’s an authorised route to the product and the volume justifies it, yes. Send the details to your account contact.

Pricing and payment terms

All topics
Do you offer volume-tiered pricing?

Yes. Unit pricing is tiered by volume, from single cartons through to full pallets.

Do you publish a trade price list?

Not publicly. Pricing moves with stock and volume, so current price lists are issued to approved accounts. Indicative pricing is often available while an enquiry is being reviewed.

How are payment terms agreed?

Terms are set when the account is opened, based on order size and trading history, and confirmed in writing before the first order.

Do you issue VAT invoices?

Yes. Every invoice carries our VAT number, GB 522 7677 76, and itemises the goods, delivery where applicable, and any duties on EU orders. Nothing is added once a quote is agreed.

Fulfilment and documentation

All topics
How are orders confirmed and fulfilled?

We confirm stock and lead time before anything is packed, then send dispatch and tracking details once the consignment is on its way.

Which territories do you ship to?

The whole of the UK, including Northern Ireland, and the EU as standard. Delivery is quoted with each order.

How are lead times confirmed?

Lead times vary by product and destination, so they’re confirmed on every order before you commit.

Which documents are supplied with each consignment?

A delivery note and invoice, plus the customs documentation required for EU consignments.

How are order issues resolved?

Directly, by your account contact: the same person who manages your orders.

Working with brands

All topics
How do you keep our pricing stable?

By controlling supply rather than fixing prices. We buy only through your approved route, supply only approved trade accounts that we can name to you, and trace every unit. Online marketplaces match the lowest price they can find; with no stock leaking to anonymous resellers, there’s nothing cheaper for them to find. When we see your range undercut, we find where that stock came from and tell you, so the leak can be closed.

Where will our range be sold?

Through approved trade accounts, retailers and wholesalers, and through online channels across the UK and EU, in the markets you agree. Every channel carries the same traced stock and your own content.

What does a distribution agreement cover?

Your approved supply route, the channels and territories we sell in, how your brand assets are used, reporting and order planning. It’s agreed in writing before we buy a single unit. Our selling prices are our own decision.

Do you support market entry for brands new to the UK?

Yes. As a UK company delivering to the EU as standard, we can act as your route into either market. You set the markets and the pace, and we build from there.

What should an initial enquiry include?

Your range and brand name, where you sell today, the markets you want to reach, how we can help (including any pricing or reseller problems), and your website. The enquiry form asks for each of these, and we’ll respond to arrange a conversation.
